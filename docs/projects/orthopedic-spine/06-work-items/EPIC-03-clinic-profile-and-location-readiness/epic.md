# Epic: Clinic profile and location readiness

**Epic Title**: Clinic profile and location readiness
**Epic Key**: EPIC-03
**Summary**: Ensure visitors can reliably find clinic details, hours, and location guidance in both launch languages.
**Labels**: mvp, clinic-profile, location, bilingual
**Priority**: Must Have
**Components**: Frontend, UX
**Fix Version**: MVP-1

---

**Epic Description:**
Problem Statement: Visitors need accurate operational details before they decide whether the clinic is reachable, relevant, and open for contact.

Objective: Launch bilingual clinic-profile content with current hours, address, contact guidance, and low-maintenance location support.

Included scope:

- Public display of address, hours, and primary contact details
- Low-maintenance location/map experience suitable for MVP
- Bilingual operational-detail coverage across launch-critical pages

Excluded scope:

- Managed social feeds or advanced location services
- Rich mapping interactions beyond the approved MVP baseline
- Marketing-channel optimization work outside core location readiness

Related feature and requirement IDs: F-003; FR-003-01, FR-003-02, FR-003-04, NFR-003-01, NFR-X02, NFR-X03

Dependencies:

- [Project Requirements by Feature](../../01-requirements/readme.md)
- [F-003 Clinic Profile, Location, and Social Presence](../../01-requirements/f-003-clinic-profile-location-and-social-presence.md)
- [Open Questions](../../open-questions.md)
- [Prototype Brief](../../05-prototype/prototype-brief.md)

Measurable success criteria:

- Visitors can find hours, address, and primary contact details from launch-critical pages without navigation confusion.
- Location guidance works with the approved low-maintenance MVP implementation.
- Operational details remain ready for bilingual launch and monthly accuracy review.
