# Epic: Public trust and bilingual launch content

**Epic Title**: Public trust and bilingual launch content
**Epic Key**: EPIC-01
**Summary**: Deliver the bilingual public launch experience that explains services, builds trust, and guides visitors to contact actions.
**Labels**: mvp, public-experience, bilingual, trust
**Priority**: Must Have
**Components**: Frontend, UX
**Fix Version**: MVP-1

---

**Epic Description:**
Problem Statement: Prospective patients need a fast, trustworthy explanation of the clinic before they decide to contact or request care.

Objective: Launch bilingual public pages that make the clinic credible, understandable, and easy to contact from mobile-first traffic.

Included scope:

- Above-the-fold value proposition and trust cues on the public home experience
- Spanish and English launch content for core public pages and CTAs
- Visible contact and appointment-request calls to action across core public flows

Excluded scope:

- Patient portal or treatment-history access
- Long-form educational content hub or campaign landing pages
- Analytics-heavy optimization work beyond MVP launch needs

Related feature and requirement IDs: F-001; FR-001-01, FR-001-02, FR-001-03, FR-001-04, NFR-001-01, NFR-001-02, NFR-X02, NFR-X03

Dependencies:

- [Project Requirements by Feature](../../01-requirements/readme.md)
- [F-001 Public Website and Trust Experience](../../01-requirements/f-001-public-website-and-trust-experience.md)
- [Phased Roadmap](../../02-planning/phased-roadmap.md)
- [Prototype Brief](../../05-prototype/prototype-brief.md)

Measurable success criteria:

- Core public pages explain services, trust signals, and next steps in both launch languages.
- Every MVP public page includes a visible path to contact or request an appointment.
- Trust-oriented launch screens meet the approved accessibility baseline for headings, contrast, and CTA clarity.
