# Stories for Epic: Public trust and bilingual launch content

## Product Owner

### US-PO-MVP-001: Approve bilingual trust-content scope for launch

**Story ID**: US-PO-MVP-001
**Epic Link**: EPIC-01
**Priority**: Must Have
**Effort Estimate**: 3

**As a** Product Owner,
**I want to** confirm the launch-ready public content scope, trust signals, and CTA placement,
**So that** the MVP ships with a clear, bilingual path from discovery to contact intent.

**Acceptance Criteria**:

- [ ] Given the MVP public pages, when scope is reviewed, then the approved launch set covers home, services, testimonials, and contact/location CTA needs.
- [ ] Given bilingual launch scope, when content is approved, then Spanish and English variants are required for every MVP public CTA and trust-critical section.
- [ ] Given launch boundaries, when backlog scope is finalized, then patient portals, long-form education content, and analytics-heavy enhancements remain out of scope.

**Deliverables**:

- Launch scope checklist for public trust content and CTAs.
- Requirement traceability for F-001 and cross-cutting launch quality items.
- Stakeholder approval notes for bilingual content readiness.

Dependencies:

- [F-001 Public Website and Trust Experience](../../01-requirements/f-001-public-website-and-trust-experience.md)
- [Open Questions](../../open-questions.md)
- [Phased Roadmap](../../02-planning/phased-roadmap.md)

Success Metrics:

- MVP public scope is approved without unresolved Must-priority requirement gaps.
- Launch content decisions remain aligned to F-001 and bilingual-readiness expectations.

## UI/UX Designer

### US-UX-MVP-001: Design the public trust landing experience

**Story ID**: US-UX-MVP-001
**Epic Link**: EPIC-01
**Priority**: Must Have
**Effort Estimate**: 5

**As a** UI/UX Designer,
**I want to** design the bilingual public landing and trust-oriented sections,
**So that** prospective patients can understand the clinic quickly and feel confident taking the next step.

**Acceptance Criteria**:

- [ ] Given a mobile-first visitor, when the public landing view loads, then the value proposition, credibility cues, and primary CTA are visible without navigation friction.
- [ ] Given testimonial and trust sections, when layouts are reviewed, then headings, reading order, and contrast align to WCAG 2.2 AA expectations.
- [ ] Given bilingual launch flows, when the design handoff is reviewed, then equivalent trust and CTA hierarchy exists in Spanish and English variants.

**Deliverables**:

- Wireframes for public home, services, and trust-oriented CTA sections.
- Accessibility annotations for testimonial, trust, and CTA patterns.
- Bilingual content layout guidance for MVP launch screens.

Dependencies:

- [F-001 Public Website and Trust Experience](../../01-requirements/f-001-public-website-and-trust-experience.md)
- [User Personas](../../user-personas.md)
- [Prototype Brief](../../05-prototype/prototype-brief.md)

Success Metrics:

- The design supports fast scanning, trust recognition, and CTA discovery on mobile.
- MVP public trust layouts are implementation-ready for bilingual release.
