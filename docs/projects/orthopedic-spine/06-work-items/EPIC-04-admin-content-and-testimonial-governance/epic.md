# Epic: Admin content and testimonial governance

**Epic Title**: Admin content and testimonial governance
**Epic Key**: EPIC-04
**Summary**: Enable admins to maintain public content and publish testimonials only after explicit approval.
**Labels**: mvp, admin, content-governance, testimonials
**Priority**: Must Have
**Components**: Backend, Frontend
**Fix Version**: MVP-1

---

**Epic Description:**
Problem Statement: Clinic stakeholders need simple admin workflows for public-content maintenance without risking unapproved testimonial publication.

Objective: Deliver lightweight admin content operations and testimonial moderation that stay inside the approved MVP scope.

Included scope:

- Admin maintenance of services, testimonials, and patient-facing clinic content
- Explicit testimonial approval before publication
- Bilingual content maintenance within the MVP admin scope

Excluded scope:

- Full marketing automation or complex review workflows
- Broad operational management beyond approved content and inquiry tasks
- Long approval chains or enterprise-grade content tooling

Related feature and requirement IDs: F-004; FR-004-01, FR-004-02, FR-004-03, FR-004-04, NFR-004-01, NFR-004-02, NFR-X05

Dependencies:

- [Project Requirements by Feature](../../01-requirements/readme.md)
- [F-004 Admin Content and Testimonial Management](../../01-requirements/f-004-admin-content-and-testimonial-management.md)
- [Phased Roadmap](../../02-planning/phased-roadmap.md)
- [API Contract](../../03-architecture/api/api-contract.md)

Measurable success criteria:

- Admin users can complete routine content updates within the approved simplicity target.
- Testimonial publication requires visible approval state before release.
- MVP admin content workflows remain bilingual-ready and do not expand into full operations management.
